Black Forest — how to win

You are Crimson. Steal the Azure diamond and hold it in your Designated Vault for 10 seconds to win. If a carrier dies, that diamond stays on the ground where they fell. Only Crimson can return the Crimson diamond: carry it into your vault and it snaps back to its cell at once, with no hold. Enemies can steal it again before that. Azure wins the same way with yours.

  • WASD move. Right-click also walks. On a phone or tablet, drag the stick at the lower left. It sits in from the corner and moves you in any direction. Keys still win when they are held.
  • Swings follow movement, including Fire Sword. Stop and you keep that facing. J, left-click, or the Attack button.
  • Before the match, equip exactly three of the six skills. Only those three buttons appear, and only they can be cast. Press the number printed on the button.
  • Fireball flies along your facing, the same facing as a swing. A diamond throw follows that facing and lands 1m in front of you. Lightning dashes along that facing. Its strike is wide along the whole path. Anyone hit cannot move for 1 second, and can still cast Fireball. For 5 seconds their skill casts take twice as long.
  • Mega Explosion, key 5 when it is one of your three, channels for 2.5 seconds. You cannot move, attack, or use other skills. A pale red disk with a bold red ring marks a blast centered 7m ahead, radius 5m, so the near edge is 2m in front of you. It deals 200 HP to everyone inside, teammates included. It costs 70 MP. The 30s cooldown shortens with INT, the same way other cooldowns do.
  • Lightsaber Beam, key 6 when it is one of your three, channels for 1.5 seconds. You cannot move, attack, or use other skills. A pale yellow rectangle with a bold yellow border runs 7.5m straight ahead and is 2m wide. It deals 75 HP to everyone in that lane, teammates included. It costs 50 MP. The 24s cooldown shortens with INT, the same way other cooldowns do.
  • Sword is 0.5m, 120°, 10 HP. V bare hand, 5 HP.
  • 1 Fireball 2 Ice Shadowing 3 Fire Sword 4 Lightning dash 5 Mega Explosion 6 Lightsaber Beam, when that skill is one of your three.
  • While you carry the diamond you cannot swing or cast, and you move at half speed. Defense appears beside Throw. Tap it to raise a shield that halves incoming damage and spends 5 MP each second. Tap again to drop it. It ends at 0 MP or when the diamond leaves you.
  • F, right-click, or Throw (it appears beside your skills) throws the diamond along your facing. It lands 1m in front of you.
  • E uses a catapult, ladder, spring, sewer, or tower hole, and repairs your gate.
  • Space hops.
  • Every river can be walked at half move speed. A short zigzag river runs in front of each castle and stops before the north and south walls, so the gate stays dry. The center is a pond, wide through the middle and pinched into a bridge at the top and a bridge at the bottom. Islands sit in the water. Cross either bridge at full speed, or walk through the pond at half speed.
  • A round tower stands on each side of each bridge, four in all, with a 1m radius. Stand at its door for 3 seconds in a row to capture it for your team. Only that team can go inside. The tower has 200 HP. If enemies break it, it stays unusable for 20 seconds, then rebuilds neutral. Inside a tower your team holds, Fireball travels farther.
